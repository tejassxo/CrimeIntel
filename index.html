Data Governance & Verification

Intelligence Methodology & Provenance

1. National Crime Records & MHA I4C NCRP Data

Citizen complaints and financial loss numbers reflect official filings from the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP), and National Citizen Helpline 1930 records (2020 through July 2026).

2. Technical Incident Disclosures & CERT-In Advisories

Technical security incidents, breach scopes, CVE vulnerability associations, and adversary attributions (LockBit, BlackCat, RedEcho, SideCopy, Lazarus) are cross-verified against CERT-In advisories, Parliamentary committee reports, and public regulatory disclosures.

3. ISRO Bhuvan Geo-Spatial Boundary Mapping

State and Union Territory geographical boundaries are aligned with official Survey of India / ISRO Bhuvan geospatial specifications, covering all 36 Indian states and territories with zero geopolitical boundary distortions.

4. 1930 "Golden Hour" Mathematical Model

Lien hold recovery curves are computed using exponential decay equations calibrated against real-time banking lien inter-bank hold response times reported by the Indian Financial Network (INFINET) and RBI banking ombudsman data.
