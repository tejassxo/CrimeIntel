RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

Methodology & Data Provenance Standards

1. Empirical Data Lineage

The Cyber Jagruti Observatory aggregates longitudinal cybercrime telemetry covering the period from 2020 through 2026 YTD. Data points are corroborated from official Parliamentary disclosures, Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) I4C NCRP releases, CERT-In vulnerability bulletins, and validated OSINT incident disclosures.

2. Geospatial Risk Scoring Methodology

The State Vulnerability Index (0–100) is calculated via normalized multi-factor regression: 40% weighting on financial loss density per capita, 35% on citizen complaint escalation rate, 15% on infrastructure probing frequency, and 10% on 1930 recovery latency.

3. Predictive Forecasting Principles

Projections for 2027–2028 are derived from ARIMA (Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average) modeling calibrated with a 95% confidence interval.
